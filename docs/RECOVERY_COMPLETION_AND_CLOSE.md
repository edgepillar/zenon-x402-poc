# Recovery completion and close ownership

An active recovery operation retains its admission slot through the final child
inspection. A stopped or busy child, or a synchronous parent close during that
inspection, refuses success and cannot admit a second original operation.
Inspection admission is checked again after the borrowed clock returns.

Timer cancellation must return undefined. Unknown receipts retain their known
timer handle. Synchronous expiry during timer creation or cancellation stays
latched, and closure checks the original clock window after cancellation.
The recovery close enters both known close legs once, preserves the first
failure (including an undefined throw), and never turns another receipt into
replacement cleanup authority. Its supported-promise observer uses captured
property operations and index loops, with no mutable array method lookup.
